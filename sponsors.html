01 Why sponsor Oasea

A small bet on a world of growth.

Most marketing buys you attention. Sponsoring Oasea buys you association — with patience, beauty, and a player base that'll remember you cared long after a banner ad has faded.

01

Attached to a positive game

No combat. No dark patterns. No ads. Players associate sponsors with calm, beauty, and care — emotional adjacencies money can't usually buy.

02

Forever in the credits

Permanent placement in the in-game credits screen, accessible from the main menu of every session, on every device, for as long as Oasea exists.

03

A real social post

An original, hand-crafted Instagram post welcoming you — not a stock template. Written by a human, in the voice of the game.

04

An island named for you

At Reef tier and above, a hand-tuned islands carries your name. Players will see it in the level select screen and when they land there.

05

A quarterly impact letter

A short, beautifully written letter every three months: who's playing, what's new, what we learned. Forwardable. Frame-able.

06

An asset pack for your channels

"Proud sponsor of Oasea" badges, social cut-downs, and a shared press kit you can use in pitches and decks. Made for you, ready to ship.

07

First look at new builds

Sponsors see new biomes, weather systems, and features two weeks before public release — and get to leave a fingerprint on the roadmap.

08

A seat at the table

Lagoon & Reef sponsors get a yearly 30-minute call with our team. Tell us where you're going. We'll tell you where the islands are headed.

09

Funds go to real work

100% of sponsorship revenue funds Oasea's ongoing development and a yearly donation to a coastal-restoration nonprofit. Audited, posted publicly.
