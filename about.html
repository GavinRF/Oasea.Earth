01 The Game

An eco-puzzler where you guide water and watch the magnificent return of life.

Oasea is a meditative island restoration game built around a journey from island to island. You'll shape the terrain, respond to unpredictable rain, and work with nature's systems to restore desolate landscapes.

Enter a world in dynamic transformation — barren rock becomes forest, desert becomes wetland, silence becomes song. No combat. No timers. Just you, the wind, and a world that yearns for what it used to be.

02 How it plays

Simple actions, endless outcomes.

Every drop counts and every decision ripples outward.

01 of three

Sculpt the land.

Raise peaks, dig valleys, cut channels. The terrain you shape decides where water will go.

02 of three

Guide the rain.

Intense storms and rogue clouds cross your island. Where they pass, water falls; where water collects, something grows.

03 of three

Watch life return.

Moss, then grass, then trees. Insects, then birds, then stabilized weather. The island takes it from here.

05 What's inside

Tiny islands, deep world.

Procedural islands

No two seeds the same. Every island starts broken and beautiful.

Living water

Real fluid simulation. Rivers carve. Lakes fill. Puddles evaporate.

Dynamic weather

Clouds, storms, evaporation. Mornings are misty. Dusks are warm.

Cozy pace

Save anywhere. Play for 3 minutes or 3 hours. The island waits.

Works anywhere

Desktop, tablet, phone. Just open a browser tab. That's it.

Share your island

Every island has a URL. Send it to a friend and they'll see the same place.
